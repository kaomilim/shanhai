Incense and reading the ash

香与观灰

The incense burns one stick every hour, up to 8, even while the game is closed. Nothing is decided until you read the ash: reading spends one offering per burnt stick and settles what each stick found.

香每隔1小时燃尽一支，最多8支，游戏关着也在燃。观灰之前什么都没有决定：观灰时每支燃尽的香消耗一份供品，并决定它找到了什么。

Three things a stick can find

一支香的三种结果

  • A stirring: a beast has come. Follow it from the map or from the incense card.
  • An ink trace: the stick found nothing this time. Traces are saved and become lingzhi.
  • A circling shape: a rare beast is on its way and will arrive in some hours. It shows in the Coming strip.
  • 异动：有异兽来了。可以从山海图或香炉卡片循迹它。
  • 墨迹：这支香这次什么也没找到。墨迹会存下来，最后变成灵芝。
  • 盘旋的影子：有稀有异兽正在赶来，几个小时后到达，会显示在「来临」里。

Every stick has the same 50 percent chance of finding a beast. A favoured offering does not change that: it only makes its beast likelier to be the one that comes, and a mountain left quiet for long only tilts the odds towards rare beasts. The Settings entry How odds work shows the exact figures.

每支香找到异兽的几率都是百分之50，固定不变。合心意的供品不会改变它，只会让对应的异兽更可能是来的那只；一座山安静得越久，只会让稀有异兽更容易出现。设置里的「几率说明」有具体数字。

The result of your last reading stays on the incense card as the “Last ash” line, so you can read it again after the pop-up is gone. You cannot read while 6 stirrings are already waiting: follow some first.

上一次观灰的结果会一直留在香炉卡片的「上次观灰」一行，提示消失后还能再看。已有6个异动在等你时不能观灰，请先去循迹。
