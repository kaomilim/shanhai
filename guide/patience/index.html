Stillness, Tracks and the Vigil

静候、足迹与守夜

Stillness

静候

The longer a mountain is left quiet, the likelier a rare beast. Stillness grows over 48 hours: after 8 hours it is 36 percent of the way, after 24 hours 74 percent. At full stillness rare beasts are about 3 times as likely and super-rare ones 6 times.

一座山安静得越久，越容易出现稀有异兽。静候在48小时内累积：8小时后到百分之36，24小时后到百分之74。完全静候时，稀有异兽约为平时的3倍，极稀有的约为6倍。

Tracks

足迹

Burn incense in a classic on different days and you earn 1 Track a day there. From 4 Tracks a super-rare beast of that classic can appear, and at 16 the next stirring there is that beast.

在不同的日子里于同一部经里烧香，每天在那里得到1个足迹。累积到4个足迹时，那部经的极稀有异兽有机会出现；到16个时，那里的下一个异动就是它。

The Vigil

守夜

With 9 Tracks you can light a great vigil. A vigil costs 1 lingzhi and 1 offering and brings a rare beast in 8 to 24 hours, or a super-rare one in 36 to 84 hours.

累积9个足迹后可以点燃大守夜。一次守夜消耗灵芝1份、供品1份，稀有异兽在8到24小时后到来，极稀有的在36到84小时后到来。
