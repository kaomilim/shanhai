The world map

山海图

The map is made of 6 worlds: the 5 mountain classics and the Seas Classic. Each world has stops, one for each mountain or region the classic describes. Once more than one world is open, swipe sideways to change world.

山海图由6个世界组成：5部山经，加上海经。每个世界有若干站点，对应经文里的一座山或一个地区。开启不止一个世界后，左右滑动可以换世界。

What the marks mean

标记的含义

  • A glowing stop with a mark: a beast is stirring there. Tap it to follow.
  • A stop that is coming: a beast is circling. You see only that something is on its way, never what.
  • A sealed stop wears the portrait of a restored beast. A stamped stop has every beast of that mountain.
  • 带标记并发光的站点：有异兽在那里异动，点它就能循迹。
  • 「来临」的站点：有异兽正在盘旋。你只知道有东西要来，不知道是什么。
  • 已封印的站点会挂上已复原异兽的画像；盖了印的站点，说明这座山的异兽都已复原。

The Seas Classic opens when you have restored 5 beasts in each mountain classic, 25 in all. The walker on the map is only a marker of where you last stood.

在每部山经里各复原5只异兽后，海经就会开启，一共25只。地图上的行者只是标出你上次停留的位置。
