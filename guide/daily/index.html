Beast of the Day and Your Beast

每日异兽与本命神兽

Beast of the Day is one mystery beast a day. Read the clues and guess. You have 6 guesses and a solved day gives 1 millet.

每日异兽每天一只谜题异兽。读线索，猜名字。你有6次机会，猜对可得小米1份。

Your Beast is a quiz of 7 questions that finds which of 75 beasts matches you. It is on the quiz site, and the game can show your beast as the walker on the map.

本命神兽是一份有7道题的测验，从75只异兽里找出和你最像的那只。它在测验网站上，游戏里也可以让它成为地图上的行者。
